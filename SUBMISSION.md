# Nook — Micro-Connections for Campus Life

## Project Name
Nook: Micro-Connections for Campus Life

## Elevator Pitch
Nook is a zero-pressure micro-connection platform that helps students bridge the gap between feeling alone and finding community — turning crowded campus spaces into opportunities for serendipitous, low-stakes human connection.

## About the Project
Students often feel painfully disconnected in crowded campus spaces — dining halls, silent library floors, between classes — due to social friction, fear of rejection, and academic burnout. Nook addresses this by letting students "host" an open seat or table with a self-expiring timer, an intent (quick lunch, silent study, casual chat, boardgame break), a vibe badge, and an icebreaker prompt. Other students can filter open tables by intent and "pull up a chair," triggering a gentle notification ping — zero pressure to stay.

Nook also includes a Campus Vibe Map to rediscover cozy, underutilized spaces (Quiet Focus, Lively & Social, Hidden Sunlit Corner) and a Daily Grounding widget (60-second breathing pacer + mood check-in) that awards a streak and suggests whether to rest or seek a study companion.

## What it does
- **Find a Nook**: Filterable cards of open tables/study sparks nearby.
- **Host a Table**: Drop a pin with intent, seats, timer, vibe, and icebreaker.
- **Campus Vibes**: Interactive map of buildings by sentiment.
- **My Sparks**: Track joined and hosted connections.
- **Daily Grounding**: 60s reflection, mood check-in, streak.

## UX Process
- **Problem**: Social friction, fear of rejection, academic burnout.
- **Research**: 74% of students want to talk to peers in common areas but don't know who is approachable.
- **Persona**: Alex, 2nd-year transfer student eating lunch alone while scrolling phone.
- **Journey**: Lonely → open Nook → filter → pull up chair → ping → low-pressure chat → grounding streak.
- **Design Decisions**: Self-expiring timers, vibe badges, icebreakers, warm sand/terracotta/sage palette, zero-pressure language.

## Results
A fully interactive, responsive single-page web app with all core flows working without page reloads. Deployed live and recorded demo.
