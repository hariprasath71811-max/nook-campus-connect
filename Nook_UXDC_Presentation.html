Micro-Connections for Campus Life

Serendipity on Campus — a zero-pressure platform bridging disconnected students with peers, spaces, and their own well-being.

Theme: Human Connection

1 / 8

Problem Definition

Students feel painfully disconnected in crowded campus spaces — dining halls, silent library floors, between classes — due to:

Social Friction

Not knowing who is approachable in common areas.

Fear of Rejection

Worry that a cold approach will be awkward or unwelcome.

Academic Burnout

Isolation deepens while studying alone for long hours.

Environmental Alienation

Cozy, underutilized campus spaces go undiscovered.

Persona & Journey Map

Alex — 2nd-year transfer student. Eats lunch alone while scrolling phone. Wants connection but fears rejection and awkwardness.

Journey

  1. Feels lonely in the dining hall.
  2. Opens Nook → sees open tables nearby.
  3. Filters by intent (lunch / study).
  4. Pulls up a chair → gentle ping sent.
  5. Low-pressure chat or body doubling.
  6. Grounding check-in builds a streak.
4 / 8

Solution Concept & Core Features

Open Tables & Study Sparks

Filterable cards by intent with vibe badges and icebreakers. "Pull up a Chair" sends a gentle ping.

Host a Table

Drop a pin, set 1–3 open seats, self-expiring timer, and an icebreaker prompt.

Campus Vibe Map

Rediscover cozy, underutilized spaces by sentiment: Quiet Focus, Lively & Social, Hidden Sunlit Corner.

Daily Grounding

60-second breathing pacer + mood check-in. Awards a streak and suggests rest or reaching out.

Feasibility, Tech Stack & Impact

Tech Stack

Vanilla JS SPA · Tailwind CSS · Lucide icons · GitHub Pages hosting.

Feasibility

Single-file, no build step, no backend required for MVP demo.

Impact

Reduces social friction, builds streaks of self-care, rediscovers campus.

Privacy

First names only, ephemeral tables, zero-pressure opt-in.
